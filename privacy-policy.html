1. Introduction

SAVR ("we," "our," or "us") provides a mobile application that helps individuals and households manage shared grocery lists, calendars, and recipes (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using SAVR, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Email: hello.sheaandco@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (first/last, if provided)
  • Authentication credentials (managed securely via our authentication provider; we do not store raw passwords)

2.2 Household & App Content

To provide the App's core features, we collect and store:

  • Household membership information (which households you belong to, your role)
  • Grocery lists and list items
  • Calendar events you create
  • Recipes you save, including titles, ingredients, and instructions
  • Photos you upload for the purpose of extracting recipe information

2.3 Voice Input

If you use voice commands to add grocery items or create calendar events, your voice recording is temporarily processed to convert your speech into text and structured data. Voice audio is sent to our backend for processing and is not stored longer than necessary to complete this function.

2.4 AI Processing

SAVR uses Google's Gemini AI service to:

  • Estimate grocery prices and recommend nearby stores
  • Extract recipe details from photos you upload
  • Interpret voice commands

Data sent for these purposes (item names, list contents, recipe images, voice transcriptions) is processed by Google's Gemini API solely to generate the requested response and is subject to Google's own data handling practices for API use. We do not use this data to train our own models.

2.5 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary for the App to function and remain secure, such as device type, operating system version, and general usage/error logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide core app functionality (shared lists, calendars, recipes, household management)
  • Process AI-powered features (price estimates, recipe extraction, voice parsing)
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve and maintain the App

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Sharing Your Information

We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With household members: Grocery lists, calendar events, and recipes you add to a shared household are visible to other members of that household.
  • With service providers: We use third-party infrastructure providers to operate the App, including Supabase (database, authentication, and backend hosting) and Google Gemini API (AI processing).
  • For legal reasons: If required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers: If SAVR is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), row-level security policies restricting access to your household's data, and secure server-side handling of third-party API credentials.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and household data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal or legitimate business purposes.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information. You can manage most of your information directly within the App. To request account deletion or exercise other rights, contact us at hello.sheaandco@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SAVR is not intended for use by children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. International Users

If you use SAVR from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above, and, where appropriate, through the App.
